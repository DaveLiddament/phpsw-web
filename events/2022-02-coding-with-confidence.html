Meeting 108 · Wednesday 9 February 2022

Coding With Confidence

After the Omicron blip we're running another in person meetup in Feb! We've got a talk about coding with confidence using PHPStan, plus space for a lightning talk if anyone wishes to volunteer. We're leaving plenty of time available for networking and speaking with others again though. Covid precautions: We're asking everyone to take a lateral flow test before attending the event, and only attend if negative. We also strongly encourage the use of masks. After the talks we'll head somewhere for drinks.
