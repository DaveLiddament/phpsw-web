Meeting 28 · Wednesday 11 March 2015

WordPress beyond The Wild West

We're trying something a little different this month – a joint night with the new and upcoming WordPress usergroup, Bristol WordPress People (http://www.meetup.com/Bristol-WordPress-People/events/220865568). Do check them out and join their group if you're interested in hearing more from them! BaseKit have kindly offered to host the evening at their office, and as always we'll sort out some ales, lagers, soft drinks and snacks. Afterwards we'll head to Horts!
