Meeting 23 · Wednesday 8 October 2014

Lightning talks

As a change of pace, in October we'll be hosting a night of 5-10 minute lightning talks at BaseKit! BaseKit have kindly offered to host the evening at their office, and as always there'll be beer and pizza. Afterwards we'll head to the pub, The Apple if it's sunny, Horts if it's not! Also, do check out our new website (http://phpsw.org.uk), as well as our new sponsors; Ents24, Just Eat and Redrock without whom our events would not be possible.
