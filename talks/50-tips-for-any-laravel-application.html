Given at PHPSW · June 2026

50 Tips for Any Laravel Application

Liam Hammett

Think you’ve seen every Laravel trick? Let’s find out.
In this fast-paced live-coding session, we’ll review a Laravel codebase and uncover over a hundred practical tips. From smarter validation rules to hidden Eloquent gems, CI/CD improvements and lesser-known testing patterns. Each example is something you can apply immediately to make your code cleaner, faster and more maintainable.

Whether you’ve been writing Laravel for a week or a decade, you’ll have at least a dozen “wait – you can do that?!” moments.
